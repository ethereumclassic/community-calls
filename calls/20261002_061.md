## Guest

This week we welcome Edilson Osório of [OriginalMy](https://originalmy.com), a Brazilian e-governance platform built to fight bureaucracy and fraud. OriginalMy uses Ethereum Classic to anchor proofs of identity, digital signatures, and document authenticity, with the aim of making them strong enough to hold up in court.

The project began in 2016 collecting signatures for citizen-proposed bills in Brazil, and moved to Ethereum Classic for its immutability. Its tools include PACWeb for preserving web evidence, Open Prescription for digital medical prescriptions, and Hääl, a protocol for secret voting on public blockchains. Background: [When Ethereum Classic Makes Social Impact Beyond the Currency](https://medium.com/classic-is-coming/when-ethereum-classic-makes-social-impact-beyond-the-currency-36e1a5147e96).
